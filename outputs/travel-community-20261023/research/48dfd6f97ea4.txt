THE MANDALA IS A COMMUNITY OF COMMUNITIES.
An affiliated collective of places and spaces created for a discerning, sophisticated and astute group.
​
Those who value the Mandala’s unique philosophy appreciate the consistency we deliver across everything we do—expect seamlessly curated and impeccable service, insistence on respect for purpose, place, responsible citizenship and sustainability.
​
Put simply, a canvas for the company you want to keep.
​
Life is an adventure. Or, at least, we at Mandala Club, would like to think it is. And we also think that an adventure is best experienced with great people in great environments.
​
Which is why we make such extraordinary efforts to create a community that inspires meaningful moments, and to make connections that matter. All while having a fabulous time.
DISCOVER
Enter the stunning heritage shop front of Mandala, where two distinct spaces set the tone for your day.. and night.
​
By day, The Members Bar is bathed in natural light—a sophisticated setting for morning catch-ups, long lunches, or martini-fuelled meetings. As the sun sets, the energy shifts, The Velvet Room comes to life behind velvet-drapes—a low-lit, mood-rich enclave made for mischief and nights that run late.
THE MEMBERS BAR
& THE VELVET ROOM
The heartbeat of the Club, POPI's is our social, vibrant, fun, stylish and even eccentric place to be, where you can experience great food, drinks, art & music. Off the side of our casual dining restaurant, you'll find our purpose-built Analogue Room, complete with a 1960s analogue sound system and enviable vintage record collection.
Plus, tucked just beyond the buzz is SIDEBAR—a stylish, spirited hideaway designed for pre-dinner aperitifs, late-night rendezvous, or intimate celebrations.
POPI'S, THE SIDEBAR
& THE ANALOGUE ROOM
The main dining room on Level 3 is The Parlour—a reimagined space designed for day-to-night living at the Club. Open from morning through late, The Parlour will offer a flexible work and lounge environment, a rotating fresh salad bar for light, nourishing lunches, and a full Club Menu of Mandala favourites—comforting, familiar, and satisfying.
Plus, tucked away in a corner is BAR MORI—the Club’s newest Japanese concept: part izakaya, part disco daydream, and all kinds of delicious, serving up classics including fresh sashimi, signature rolls, and elevated small plates that pair perfectly with your evening pour.
