Become A Member

Membership to Carlyle & Co. isn’t for everyone, it’s for those seeking to be a part of a truly unique community, individuals that delight in making new connections and love making the most of life.

Does that sound like you? Excellent.

 

There are a number of Membership options available, including adjusted rates for applicants aged 33 years old and below, plus an optional supplement for access to the wellness facilities on Club 53.

 

Please note, that each Membership application is available on an enquiry basis only and applications incur a non-refundable $200 processing fee.
