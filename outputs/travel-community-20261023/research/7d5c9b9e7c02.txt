Modernist AI & FARR Summit 2026
Tickets/More Info
This year we're partnering with the team behind TEDai to produce our next Summit, with a focus on Frontier AI, Autonomy, Research, & Robotics. (FARR)
​The FARR Summit, created & led by members
Soha & Suzanne Zadeh
, brings together a carefully curated group of founders, researchers, executives, investors, and global leaders shaping the next era of technology.
​The experience will feature keynotes, intimate conversations, technology demonstrations, and thoughtfully curated connections inside an immersive setting.
​Attendance is private, invitation-only, and subject to approval. Each registration is reviewed individually to preserve the quality and relevance of the community. Confirmations are non-transferable.
