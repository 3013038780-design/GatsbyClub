Our Story

Founded by entrepreneur sharan pasricha, estelle community exists to cultivate a global community rooted in discretion, sophistication and timelessness, where true luxury is felt rather than seen.

In an era shaped by artificial intelligence, we champion the value of being present, a quiet return to the here and now.

We bring together a community of people with plenty to say, but nothing to prove, through extraordinary spaces and experiences around the world that prioritise genuine human connection over digital noise.

That community comes to life across a collection of hosted homes. First gathering at Maison Estelle in Mayfair, a Grade I-listed Georgian townhouse, it has since expanded to Estelle Manor in Oxfordshire, a Grade II-listed landmark house set within an expansive estate and surrounding parkland, and Celeste in London’s Notting Hill, a meticulously restored townhouse set within its vibrant neighbourhood.

Each property has its own character, all sharing the same free-spirited, unstuffy soul.
