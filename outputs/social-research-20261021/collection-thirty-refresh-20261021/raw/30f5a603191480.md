### **EXCLUSIVE OFFER FOR RBSC & RBSC POLO CLUB MEMBERS**
#### **Access to Roosevelt Private Members’ Club and restaurant**
  * Cigar Lounge
  * Library
  * Roosevelt Rooftop Terrace with panoramic the Bund views
  * Private Chinese Wine Cellar Experience


Members may arrange private visits and usage of one of the largest Chinese wine cellars in China, for wine tastings, private entertaining, or themed gatherings (advance reservation required).
  * Customized Dining Experience


May request bespoke menus arranged in advance, including but not limited to:
  * Chinese Cuisine (Shanghai & Cantonese Cuisine）
  * Western Cuisine
  * Fusion Cuisine


All customized menus will be tailored according to members’ preferences, event themes, and guest numbers, subject to prior consultation and confirmation.
#### **10% Service Charge Waiver**
A 10% service charge waiver at Roosevelt Private Members’ Club.
#### **Priority Reservation for Scenic Seating at Roosevelt Steakhouse**
Priority access to reserve scenic seating at Roosevelt Steakhouse, subject to availability.
All privileges are subject to availability and club policies. Roosevelt Private Members’ Club reserves the right of final interpretation.
