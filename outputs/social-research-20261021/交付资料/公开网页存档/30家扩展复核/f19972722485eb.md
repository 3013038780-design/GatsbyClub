# For over [_110 years_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/110-years), AmCham Shanghai has been a dynamic and thriving business service organization dedicated to the success and growth of our members. From the smallest of start-ups to the largest global multinational corporations, we provide members with a wealth of [_programs_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/events/upcoming), [_services_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/services) and [_resources_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/resources/publications)**.**
![](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/imaginary/resize/800/sites/default/files/inline-images/Screenshot%202026-01-20%20at%2011.49.18%E2%80%AFAM_0.png)
# Our members come from around the globe and represent a diverse set of industries. We operate 20 different [_industry committees_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/committees/about-committees) to bring our members the latest and most relevant information for their businesses. We also host more than 300 [_events_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/events/upcoming) per year including policy briefings, conferences, trainings and workshops to bring together members, experts and other professionals to network, learn and share insights.

## **Members benefit from:**
**-**[_**Networking Opportunities**_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/events/upcoming) >
Connect with fellow members and industry leaders to share insights and strategies. 
**-**[_**Exclusive Resources**_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/resources/publications) >
Gain access to specialized reports, research, insights and best practices that support informed decision-making.
**-**[_**Educational Programs**_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/events/training) >
Participate in workshops, seminars, and events aimed at enhancing leadership skills and industry knowledge.
**-**[_**Advocacy and Representation**_](https://www.amcham-shanghai.org/en/advocacy-initiatives) >
Benefit from a collective voice on key issues impacting the business community.
## **Member-Only Services**
In partnership with established and trusted companies, AmCham Shanghai offers a wide range of services exclusive to our members, including Medical Benefits Program, Conference Room Rental, Executive Briefing, Corporate Visa Program and more.
