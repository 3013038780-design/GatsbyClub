# [Why Become a Member](https://thecapitalclub.com/membership/ "Why Become a Member")
The benefits of joining a private club like the Capital Club are countless.
We do our best to exceed your expectations and provide members with an international environment both at the club and when they are traveling abroad. We have an extensive affiliate & associate club network which spans over 250 private clubs, business clubs, golf clubs & resorts worldwide which Capital Club members have access to simply by showing their membership card.
Members are able to associate themselves with both the Chinese and foreign business leaders of Beijing, through the opportunities created by the Club organized events and the casual gatherings in the Club bars and other facilities.
The Capital Club recognizes that every prospective Member’s situation is unique, therefore it is important that the Capital Club is a place where members can gather to entertain, relax and network in their choice of sumptuous and handsomely furnished dining rooms and meeting rooms in total privacy.
The benefits of having Capital Club membership include dining privileges in the Western and Chinese dining rooms where the tables are spaced to ensure private conversations stay that way, the use of nine private dining/conference rooms free of charge, invitation to all Club events, use of the Club’s Athletic facilities and a personalized service, right from facial recognition at reception to creating events which specifically interest you.
