July 9 & 10, 2026 / GMT+8 Add to Calendar Add this event to any of these calendars iCal> Outlook> Google> Yahoo> | Shanghai 2026 Summer Youth Program (2 Days) 上海美国商会暑期青少年项目 Committees Event Education Committee Home Venue Home The American Chamber of Commerce in Shanghai is hosting a Summer Youth Program from July 9 to 10 . This two-day program offers students in grades 10 to 12 the opportunity to learn about how multinational companies operate and grow business in China market. They will engage with business leaders, visit multinational companies, and gain knowledge of the global business environment and industry developments. The program is highly immersive and interactive, encouraging young leaders to ask questions and think about their future careers. 上海美国商会将于7月9日至10日举办暑期青少年项目。为期两天的项目旨在为美国商会会员子女（10至12年级学生）提供了解跨国企业如何在中国市场经营和增长的机会。他们将有机会与商界领袖面对面，参观跨国公司，体验商务活动，了解行业发展的最新趋势。项目具有很强的互动性，我们鼓励未来的领导者带着好奇和问题而来，也希望这个沉浸式项目能启发他们对下一程学习和未来事业的思考。欢迎报名！ Time: July 9 and 10, 9:30 am - 5:00 pm Venue: The American Chamber of Commerce in Shanghai Conference Center Language: English ★ A review of past Summer Youth Program ★ 2025 Summer Youth Program Recap​ ​ 2024 Summer Youth Program Recap​ ​ ﻿Registration Details: · The Summer Youth Program is open to children of the Chamber members or employees of member companies . · Attendees must be students in Grades 10-12 · The cost of participation covers lunch and transportation during the program · If you need to cancel your registration, please notify kelly.deng@amcham-shanghai.org before July 3, 2026, at 12 p.m .
