EO RISE 4 — Building Brands That Become Lifestyles

13 November 2026 09:00 AM (GMT+07:00) Bangkok, Hanoi, Jakarta
Venue: TBD
Location: Vietnam

Randy Dobson built CMG.ASIA from a single fitness club in 2007 into a $200M+ wellness and lifestyle ecosystem, then did it again with M.O.I Cosmetics and GAM Entertainment, Vietnam's #1 League of Legends team.

At RISE 4, he breaks down category creation, building a market instead of competing in one that already exists.

His 90-minute session opens the morning, interactive: the room drafts their own category-creation angle, then compares notes with the founders nearby. A 30-minute panel and a 20-minute peer-sharing close round out the rest.

Part of EO Vietnam's RISE series, open to members and founders who aren't members yet.

Time: 9.00 AM to 12.00 PM

Venue: TBD

Free for EO Vietnam members. US$200 for guests.
